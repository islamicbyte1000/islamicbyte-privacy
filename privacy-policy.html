This Privacy Policy explains what information the Islamic Byte mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The App is published by [Md. Abdul Hadie], Taltoli, Barguna, Barishal 8710, Bangladesh. By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Summary. You can use most of the App (Quran, duas, prayer times, tasbih, Qibla, trackers) without an account. An account is needed only for the referral and rewards features. We do not sell your personal data, we do not collect your GPS location, and your prayer/tasbih/Ramadan trackers stay on your phone.

1. Information we collect

1.1 If you create an account (optional)

  • Name, address and district that you type in.
  • Gmail address — used to sign in and to verify your account through a confirmation link sent to your inbox.
  • Mobile number — you provide it when registering. It is stored with your account and used for prize delivery/contact and to prevent duplicate accounts. It is not verified by SMS at registration.
  • Password — handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Confirmation that you are 18 or older and that you accepted our terms.

1.2 Referral and rewards features

  • Your referral code, the code that invited you (if any), install-referrer information provided by Google Play, and how many days you were active (used to credit referral coins).
  • A device identifier hash: a one-way hashed value derived from your Android ID. We use it only to allow one referral per device and to prevent fraud. We cannot reverse it into your Android ID.
  • Your coin balance, daily reward claims and streak counters that are linked to your account.
  • If you request a reward: the request type, and the name, email, phone and address from your profile, so that we can deliver it.

1.3 Top list (leaderboard) — only if you choose

If you tick the option to appear in the Top list, your name, district, earned coins and referral count are visible to other users of the App. You can turn this off at any time in Profile & Settings.

1.4 Quizzes and contests

If you submit a quiz entry we collect your name, mobile number, address, device ID, quiz round and score. The mobile number is verified by a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS through Google Firebase Phone Authentication, and the quiz entry is used to select and contact winners.

1.5 Advertising data

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show ads and measure performance. The App declares the Google Play "advertising ID" permission for this purpose.

1.6 Data that stays only on your device

These are stored locally on your phone and are not sent to us: prayer tracker, tasbih counts, daily streak history, Ramadan fasting tracker, Quran completion (khatam) plan, Quran bookmarks and last-read position, selected city, settings, and cached content. Clearing app data or uninstalling the App removes them.

1.7 Sensors and location

  • The Qibla compass uses your phone's compass (magnetometer) sensor locally. Sensor readings are not collected or sent anywhere.
  • We do not request or collect your GPS/precise location. Prayer, sehri and iftar times are calculated from the city you select in the App, and the Qibla direction is calculated from that city.

1.8 What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data, payment/card information, or biometric data. We do not ask for or store your religion. The App currently does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and does not send push notifications.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in, and keep one active session per account.
  • To run referrals, daily rewards, coins, the Top list, quizzes, and reward requests.
  • To prevent duplicate accounts, abuse and fraud.
  • To show ads (through Google AdMob) which helps keep the App free.
  • To respond to your messages and to meet legal obligations.

4. Advertising choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). You can also see Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account data while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account inside the App: Referral Reward → Profile & Settings → Delete Account (you will be asked for your password). This permanently deletes your profile, coin balance, registered phone/email entries, referral code, Top-list entry and sign-in account.
  • Certain records — referral records, reward requests and quiz entries — may be kept for a limited time after deletion where needed to deliver prizes, prevent fraud, resolve disputes or meet legal requirements, and are then deleted or anonymised.
  • You can also request deletion by email — see Delete account. Backups may take a short additional time to clear.

6. Security

We use Google Firebase security features, access rules that restrict each user to their own data, and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The App's content can be read by anyone, but accounts, referrals and rewards are only for people 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for accounts. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Google's servers that process Firebase and AdMob data may be located outside Bangladesh. By using the App you understand that your information may be processed in other countries.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, or to object to certain processing. You can edit your name, address, district and Top-list setting inside the App, and delete your account as described above. For any other request, email us.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If changes are significant we will show a notice in the App or on this page.
